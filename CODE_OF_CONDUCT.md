# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributors, users, and adopters from every background. Minervit
serves as steward of this community and is committed to keeping participation harassment-free,
respectful, and productive for everyone. This policy applies to all project spaces and to any
conduct that affects the community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and welcoming toward others.
- Assume good faith, and give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project, not personal advantage.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experience levels, and decisions, even in disagreement.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, apologize when you cause harm, and learn from it.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing others' private information (such as a physical or email address) without
  explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, deliberate trolling, or other conduct that a
  reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — including the repository, issues,
pull requests, discussions, and chat — and whenever an individual is representing the project
or its community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may
remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, or other interactions that violate
this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation by anyone whose
behavior is deemed inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Maintainers will act fairly and consistently, respect the privacy and security of anyone who
reports an incident, and apply consequences in proportion to the violation. Decisions are made
at the discretion of the maintainers acting as stewards on behalf of Minervit.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to **conduct@minervit.ai**.
All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and confidentially. You will not be
penalized in good faith for raising a concern.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is an original policy maintained by Minervit. It is informed by widely
adopted open-source community standards, including the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
